You arrive at the harbour market with a cart, a little coin and one good order to fill. Haggle in plain words with the merchants on the quay. Each keeps their own books, hears their own rumours, and remembers how you treated them.

  • Fill your contract before the market closes, or it pays nothing.
  • Type deals the way you'd say them: 3 dye for 2 wool, how much for your amber?, any news?, give me 2 iron now, I'll pay 15 coin on day 4.
  • You score your profit over what your stall was worth on the first morning.
